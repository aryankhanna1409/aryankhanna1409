I started with robots and kept moving up the stack. Every project here is one pour of the same brew: a robot **moves**, a vision model **sees**, a retrieval system **knows** (and cites its sources), an agent **acts** (with a human in the loop), and a product **ships**. What each stage learns feeds the next.

<picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="assets/loop-light.svg" /><img src="assets/loop.svg" width="100%" alt="The loop: grind (move, autonomous-bot), bloom (see, sentinel), steep (know, finintel), pour (act, nexus), serve (ship, dishdecoded)" /></picture>

<br>

<a href="https://github.com/aryankhanna1409/autonomous-bot"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="assets/projects/autonomous-bot-light.svg" /><img src="assets/projects/autonomous-bot.svg" width="100%" alt="01 Grind · MOVE · autonomous-bot: a ROS 2 robot that maps, localises and navigates on its own, with an IoT dashboard and a swarm auction" /></picture></a>

<sub>IMU fused through an error-state Kalman filter, 2D SLAM with slam_toolbox, Nav2 path planning, a live browser dashboard over MQTT, and a swarm auction on OpenRMF where every robot bids its total ETA. Built as a team for warehouse and hospital logistics.</sub>

<br>

<a href="https://github.com/aryankhanna1409/sentinel"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="assets/projects/sentinel-light.svg" /><img src="assets/projects/sentinel.svg" width="100%" alt="02 Bloom · SEE · sentinel: search-and-rescue vision that finds people who are hidden or in the dark" /></picture></a>

<div align="center"><a href="https://github.com/aryankhanna1409/sentinel"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aryankhanna1409/sentinel/main/assets/demo.gif" alt="Sentinel HUD demo" width="78%" /></a></div>

<sub>A YOLO11 detector trained for crowded, occluded scenes, with tracking, a radar view and a casualty cue for people lying still. The thermal view runs on a simulated feed today. A real thermal camera plugs into the same loop and works straight away.</sub>

<br>

<a href="https://github.com/aryankhanna1409/finintel"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="assets/projects/finintel-light.svg" /><img src="assets/projects/finintel.svg" width="100%" alt="03 Steep · KNOW · finintel: a financial RAG system that writes grounded, citation-backed advisory reports from SEC filings" /></picture></a>

<div align="center"><a href="https://github.com/aryankhanna1409/finintel"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aryankhanna1409/finintel/main/assets/demo.gif" alt="finintel demo" width="78%" /></a></div>

<sub>Ingests SEC filings, 13F holdings, insider trades and earnings calls, retrieves with BM25 plus dense search, and refuses to answer without citing evidence. A prototype that covers a small set of companies so far: the worked example benchmarks Target against Walmart, Costco, Dollar General and Kroger.</sub>

<br>

<a href="https://github.com/aryankhanna1409/nexus"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="assets/projects/nexus-light.svg" /><img src="assets/projects/nexus.svg" width="100%" alt="04 Pour · ACT · nexus: network intelligence and a policy-gated DevSecOps agent" /></picture></a>

<div align="center"><a href="https://github.com/aryankhanna1409/nexus"><img src="assets/demos/nexus.gif" alt="nexus demo" width="78%" /></a></div>

<sub>A prototype for a simulated telecom network. A C library parses call records, monitoring watches 17 health metrics per tower, and a root-cause engine lines alarms up against recent deployments. The AI can investigate and explain, but fixes wait for a human to approve them.</sub>

<br>

<a href="https://github.com/aryankhanna1409/dishdecoded"><picture><source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="assets/projects/dishdecoded-light.svg" /><img src="assets/projects/dishdecoded.svg" width="100%" alt="05 Serve · SHIP · dishdecoded: snap a dish or a menu and get its story, allergens and a recipe" /></picture></a>

<div align="center"><a href="https://github.com/aryankhanna1409/dishdecoded"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aryankhanna1409/dishdecoded/main/assets/demo.gif" alt="DishDecoded demo" width="46%" /></a></div>

<sub>Seven features on one engine: dish identifier, allergen check, pairings, food culture, recipe, menu decoder and a pantry mode. One vision call per request, validated against a schema, with a free demo mode.</sub>
